# ZINNIA 2026: Official Symposium Handbook & Information Guide

## 1. Overview and Institutional Heritage
ZINNIA 2026 is the premier National Level Technical Symposium organized by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Government College of Engineering, Erode (GCE Erode, formerly known as IRTT). Established in 1984 under the aegis of the Institute of Road and Transport Technology, GCE Erode has stood at the forefront of engineering excellence for over four decades. The college spans a sprawling 350-acre green campus located on the Salem-Kochi National Highway (NH-544) in Chithode, Erode, Tamil Nadu.

## 2. Key Symposium Highlights
- **Flagship Event**: Zinnia 2026 brings together top engineering talent from universities and colleges across India.
- **Competitions**: 9 rigorous technical and non-technical challenges designed to test problem-solving, AI prowess, database administration, system design, creative storytelling, and campus tactical exploration.
- **Prize Pool**: Over ₹30,000+ in cash awards, accompanied by trophies, merit badges, and shields.
- **Recognition**: Official Certificates of Participation recognized under Anna University for all registered attendees.
- **Digital Infrastructure**: Automated QR verification, Agent ID Passport passes, and instant attendance and food management.

## 3. Registration Details & Pricing
- **Registration Fee**: Exactly ₹150 per participant.
- **What is Included**:
  - Unrestricted entry to compete in selected non-clashing events.
  - Official Zinnia 2026 welcome kit.
  - Delicious complimentary South Indian buffet lunch and tea/snacks.
  - Anna University recognized verified participation certificate.
  - Access to all symposium tracks, gaming, and guest lectures.
- **Registration Procedure**:
  - Online pre-registration is available via the official portal at `/register`.
  - Fill in Name, Email, Phone, College Name, Department, Year, and select the events.
  - Upon submission, a unique holographic **Agent ID** (e.g. `ZIN26-A8F41C`) is generated.
  - Save or screenshot the Digital Passport for barcode scanning at campus terminals.
  - On-the-spot registration is open at the Main Desk from 8:30 AM to 9:30 AM on the day of the symposium.
- **Deadlines**: Online registrations close 24 hours prior to the symposium. Spot registrations are subject to venue capacity.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
- All bona fide undergraduate (B.E. / B.Tech) and postgraduate (M.E. / M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc CS/IT) students of any accredited college or university are eligible.
- Cross-college and cross-departmental teams are permitted for team events.
- Physical college ID card or authorized bonafide letter from the institution is mandatory for campus entry.

## 5. Accommodation and Hospitality
- Separate hostel accommodation for male and female outstation participants is provided inside the campus hostel blocks upon prior notification.
- Outstation students must contact the hospitality desk (+91 94567 89012) at least 2 days in advance to reserve beds.
- Hygienic dining arrangements and pure drinking water stations are available across all event blocks.

## 6. Certificates and Prizes
- Every student who attends and participates in events is awarded a physical Certificate of Participation.
- Winners of 1st, 2nd, and 3rd prizes in every event receive cash awards and Certificate of Merit during the grand Valedictory function at the Main Auditorium.
- Total prize money distribution is verified and handed out in cash/digital transfer along with physical trophies and shields.
