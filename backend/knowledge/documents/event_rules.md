# ZINNIA 2026: Official Event Rules, Formats & Guidelines

This document outlines the operational specifications, rules, constraints, judging criteria, and prizes for each of the 9 events organized at Zinnia 2026.

---

## 1. Technical Events

### MSN-01: Operation System Recovery (Debugging)
- **Category**: Technical
- **Clearance**: Level 01
- **Team Size**: 1 to 2 members
- **Timing**: 10:00 AM - 10:45 AM (Duration: 45 minutes)
- **Venue**: Cyber Lab 01, Newton Hall
- **Format**:
  - Round 1: Syntax errors, compiler warning resolution, and formatting flaws.
  - Round 2: Logical bugs, algorithmic discrepancies, and edge-case mishandling.
  - Round 3: Memory leaks, pointer arithmetic issues, and runtime exceptions.
- **Rules & Environment**:
  - Languages supported: C, C++, Java, and Python.
  - Standard IDEs (GCC, VS Code, IntelliJ, PyCharm) provided on lab terminals.
  - External internet access is disabled during the rounds; official offline language documentation is provided.
  - Ranking is determined strictly by the number of passing test cases and completion timestamp.
- **Prizes**: 1st: ₹3,000 + CHRONOS Merit Badge | 2nd: ₹2,000 + Certificate | 3rd: ₹1,000 + Certificate.

### MSN-02: Operation ORACLE (AI & Prompt Engineering Challenge)
- **Category**: Technical
- **Clearance**: Level 02
- **Team Size**: 1 to 2 members
- **Timing**: 11:15 AM - 12:15 PM (Duration: 1 hour)
- **Venue**: AI Research Arena, Turing Block
- **Format**:
  - Phase 1: Zero-shot / Few-shot prompt engineering to extract structured deterministic outputs from noisy data streams.
  - Phase 2: Model anomaly detection, hallucination mitigation, and adversarial jailbreak vulnerability patching.
- **Rules**:
  - Evaluated on precision, token efficiency, deterministic consistency, and robustness.
- **Prizes**: 1st: ₹3,500 + AI Innovator Badge | 2nd: ₹2,000 + Runner-up Badge | 3rd: ₹1,000 + Recognition Certificate.

### MSN-03: Operation Broken Records (Lost in SQL)
- **Category**: Technical
- **Clearance**: Level 01
- **Team Size**: 1 to 2 members
- **Timing**: 12:30 PM - 01:30 PM (Duration: 1 hour)
- **Venue**: Database Systems Lab, Babbage Wing
- **Format**:
  - Complex schema analysis, table reconstruction from partial dumps, multi-table joins, subqueries, recursive CTEs, and window functions.
- **Rules**:
  - Dialects supported: PostgreSQL 16 and MySQL 8.0.
  - Queries evaluated on correctness, output accuracy, and query execution plan cost / execution speed.
- **Prizes**: 1st: ₹3,000 + Data Archon Shield | 2nd: ₹2,000 + Excellence Certificate | 3rd: ₹1,000 + Merit Certificate.

### MSN-04: Operation Infinity Protocol (Grand Marathon Hackathon)
- **Category**: Technical (Exclusive Track)
- **Clearance**: Level 03
- **Team Size**: 2 to 3 members strictly
- **Timing**: 10:00 AM - 01:30 PM (Continuous Duration: 3 hours 30 minutes)
- **Venue**: Main Innovation Center
- **Format**:
  - Stage 1 (Algorithm Forge): Rapid dynamic programming and data structure algorithmic hurdles.
  - Stage 2 (System Crash Survival): Real-time crisis containment, full-stack microservice recovery, and distributed system debugging.
  - Stage 3 (Live Defense Pitch): 5-minute solution defense before the jury panel.
- **Special Rule**: Because this is a continuous marathon, participants cannot simultaneously take part in overlapping short technical rounds.
- **Prizes**: 1st: ₹6,000 + Grand Temporal Champion Trophy | 2nd: ₹4,000 + Runner-up Shield | 3rd: ₹2,000 + Certificate.

### MSN-05: Operation Mission Control (UI/UX Design)
- **Category**: Technical (Exclusive Track)
- **Clearance**: Level 02
- **Team Size**: 1 to 2 members
- **Timing**: 10:30 AM - 01:00 PM (Duration: 2 hours 30 minutes)
- **Venue**: Design & Graphics Studio, Lovelace Hub
- **Format**:
  - Design a futuristic, high-usability terminal interface or dashboard for incident command and temporal monitoring.
- **Tools**: Figma, Adobe XD, or Penpot.
- **Evaluation Criteria**: User empathy, layout hierarchy, responsive ergonomics, accessibility (WCAG), and visual aesthetics.
- **Prizes**: 1st: ₹3,500 + Creative Vanguard Trophy | 2nd: ₹2,000 + Certificate | 3rd: ₹1,000 + Certificate.

## 2. Non-Technical Events

### MSN-06: Borderland at GCE (Campus Mystery Treasure Hunt)
- **Category**: Non-Technical
- **Team Size**: 2 to 4 members
- **Timing**: 10:45 AM - 01:00 PM (Duration: 2 hours 15 minutes)
- **Venue**: Campus Quadrangle & Outdoor Checkpoints
- **Format**: Multilevel riddle decryption leading to hidden clue markers placed across the 350-acre GCE Erode campus.
- **Rules**: Strict adherence to campus boundaries; damage to plants or infrastructure leads to immediate disqualification.
- **Prizes**: 1st: ₹3,000 + Medals | 2nd: ₹2,000 + Certificates | 3rd: ₹1,000 + Certificates.

### MSN-07: Think, Strike and Win (Fast-Paced Quiz)
- **Category**: Non-Technical
- **Team Size**: 2 to 3 members
- **Timing**: 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM (Duration: 1 hour)
- **Venue**: Seminar Hall B
- **Format**: Written preliminary screening of 25 trivia questions followed by a live stage buzzer round for the top 6 teams.
- **Rules**: Negative marking applies for wrong answers in the buzzer round.
- **Prizes**: 1st: ₹2,500 + Shield | 2nd: ₹1,500 + Certificate | 3rd: ₹1,000 + Certificate.

### MSN-08: Plot Twist (Impromptu Storytelling & Drama)
- **Category**: Non-Technical
- **Team Size**: 1 to 2 members
- **Timing**: 12:00 PM - 01:00 PM (Duration: 1 hour)
- **Venue**: Media Studio / AV Hall
- **Format**: Teams receive an unexpected premise or plot interruption on the spot. 10 minutes preparation time followed by a 4-minute presentation.
- **Prizes**: 1st: ₹2,500 + Award | 2nd: ₹1,500 + Certificate | 3rd: ₹1,000 + Certificate.

### MSN-09: Short Film (Cinematic Showcase)
- **Category**: Non-Technical
- **Team Size**: 1 to 5 members
- **Timing**: 12:30 PM - 01:00 PM (Duration: 30 minutes screening)
- **Venue**: Main Auditorium Screen 01
- **Format**: Short film submissions with duration 3 to 10 minutes. Themes: AI, futuristic sci-fi, or human connection.
- **Prizes**: 1st: ₹3,000 + Golden Reel Trophy | 2nd: ₹2,000 + Runner-up Reel | 3rd: ₹1,000 + Certificate.
