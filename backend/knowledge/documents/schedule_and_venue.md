## 2. Venue Location & How to Reach

### Institution Address
**Department of Computer Science and Engineering**
Government College of Engineering, Erode (GCE Erode / Formerly IRTT)
NH-544 (Salem - Cochin Highway), Chithode, Erode, Tamil Nadu — 638316.

### Travel & Transportation
- **From Erode Central Bus Stand (Mofussil & Town Bus)**: Frequent government and town buses towards Bhavani, Gobichettipalayam, Salem, and Coimbatore pass directly in front of the GCE Erode Main Gate (Bus stop: IRTT / GCE Erode). Travel time is approximately 25-30 minutes.
- **From Erode Junction Railway Station (ED)**: Distance is approximately 14 km. Direct city buses, auto-rickshaws, and taxi services are available 24/7 from the station forecourt.
- **From Coimbatore International Airport (CJB)**: Distance is approximately 85 km via NH-544 (about 1 hour 45 minutes by taxi or express bus).
