# ZINNIA 2026 — Registration Flow Specification

## 3-Step Wizard

1. **Step 1: Agent Identification**
   - Full Name
   - Email Address (Duplicate check enforced)
   - Phone Number (10 digits validation)

2. **Step 2: Institutional Affiliation**
   - College / University Name
   - Engineering Department
   - Year of Study (I, II, III, IV, PG)

3. **Step 3: Tactical Mission Assignment**
   - Select multiple short missions or exclusive single-event marathons (e.g. Infinity Protocol or UI/UX Design).
   - Generates unique Agent ID (`ZIN26-XXXXXX`) and secure QR Token.
   - Immediate redirect to Digital Symposium Passport.
